Process preview

From fit call to supported handoff.

Trustline starts with fit and assessment, then moves through architecture, hardware planning, deployment, migration, validation, handoff, and the first support runway.

Confirm goals, migration scope, compatibility, and whether the engagement makes sense.

Build and migration

Handoff and care

Validation standards

Credible because the system is checked before handoff.

Trustline deployments are validated before handoff: services are patched and maintained, standard security practices are followed, containerization is used where appropriate, backups and restore paths are reviewed, remote access is tested, and risky AI actions stay approval-gated.
