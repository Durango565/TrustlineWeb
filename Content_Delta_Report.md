# Content Delta Report: TrustlineWeb Migration
**Target:** Migration of Sovereign ('main') language into Beta-Baseline ('beta')

## 1. Process Page Comparison

### Linguistic Framing
- **Beta Baseline:** Technical and professional. Focuses on "absolute ownership," "staged delivery," and "risk reduction."
- **Sovereign (Main):** Narrative and emotional. Focuses on "the road to owning your digital life," "the rental trap," and "taking back control."
- **Core Hook:** The 'main' version introduces a strong anti-rental narrative ("Stop renting your digital life") that transforms the service from a technical deployment to a liberation event.

### The 11-Step Path Comparison
The structure remains identical (11 steps), but the terminology has shifted from *Technical/Professional* to *Narrative/Symbolic*.

| Step | Beta (Original) | Sovereign (Main) | Shift Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 01 | Fit Call | The Fit Call | Minor refinement |
| 02 | Liberation | The Great Escape | Narrative |
| 03 | Architecture Plan | The Blueprint | Metaphor |
| 04 | Hardware Sourcing | The Gear | Simplification |
| 05 | Build & Burn-In | The Stress Test | Outcome-focused |
| 06 | Fortress Build | Building the Fortress | Active phrasing |
| 07 | Seamless Transition | Moving Day | Relatable/Domestic |
| 08 | Local AI Operator Setup | Your AI Pilot | Persona-based |
| 09 | Testing | The Final Check | Reassuring |
| 10 | Handoff | The Keys | Symbolic |
| 11 | Guardian Stewardship | The Watchman | Protective |

### Closing Logic & Framing
- **Beta:** Uses the header **"Order reduces risk"** focusing on avoiding "improvised live" deployment.
- **Sovereign:** Uses the header **"Order stops mistakes"** focusing on a definitive path: "First we plan, then we build, then we move."
- **Key Delta:** The 'main' version replaces "Risk Reduction" (fear-based/corporate) with "Mistake Prevention" (certainty-based/expert).

## 2. Contact Page Comparison

### Hero Section Copy
- **Beta:** Utility-driven. *"Book a fit call."* Focuses on the mechanism of the first meeting.
- **Sovereign:** Identity-driven. *"Claim your Pilot's Seat."* Focuses on the end state (authority and control).
- **Lede Text:** Shift from describing the "remote or on-site assessment" to emphasizing the emotional pain of the "Rental Economy."

### Form Field Evolution
The form has been updated to act as a psychological filter rather than just a data collection tool.

- **The Qualifying Question:** 
  - *Beta:* "What are you trying to replace or consolidate?" (Technical/Functional)
  - *Sovereign:* "What do you want to stop renting?" (Financial/Ownership)
- **Call to Action (Button):**
  - *Beta:* "Request a Fit Call"
  - *Sovereign:* "Claim My Pilot's Seat"

### "Is this for you?" Sidebar
The sidebar has been refined to be more opinionated, creating a stronger "us vs. them" distinction.

- **Good Fit:** Shifted from general traits ("care about privacy") to specific values ("value privacy over 'free' apps").
- **Not For You:** Shifted from "Not ideal if" to "Not for you if," and replaced "cheapest NAS" with "cheapest box" to sound less like a hardware spec and more like a value judgment.

## Migration Recommendation
**Approval Recommended.** The 'Sovereign' language significantly increases the perceived value of the service by framing it as a transition to ownership rather than a technical installation. The structural integrity of the 'Beta' version is preserved, making this a low-risk, high-impact content migration.
