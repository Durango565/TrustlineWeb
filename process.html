Process

The Setup Week, without the panic.

Most people fear the switch. We remove that risk by planning first, building the system, then moving through The Setup Week: Mirror, Validate, Switch Over.

Built for you

You don't need to be a tech expert. We do the hard work. You get the keys.

Timeline

From Private System Assessment to owner control.

01
Fit Call

We talk. We see if private infrastructure is right for your household.

02
Private System Assessment

We map your current services, subscriptions, data, and household needs. On-Site Assessment is optional for complex homes.

03
Core Infrastructure Plan

We specify the server, storage, router, battery backup, and the AI Power Tier that fits your household.

04
Hardware Sourcing

We source the hardware. You own it outright, with receipts and no mystery bundle.

05
System Build

We build, harden, and burn in your system before it touches your home.

06
Days 1-3 — Mirror

Your data is copied from your current services in the background. You notice nothing; everything you use today keeps running.

07
Days 4-5 — Validate

Every file is verified against the original, byte by byte. We do not move on until the copy is provably complete.

08
Days 6-7 — Switch Over

Services come online one at a time, on your schedule. Each is confirmed working on your devices before the next begins.

09
AI Operator Handoff

We set up your AI Operator and show you how to ask about files, backups, health, and household state.

10
The Keys

We hand over the keys, documentation, and operating model. You have owner control.

11
Guardian Care

Included and required through your commissioning period to tune the estate to your household. After that, optional monthly care — updates, patches, monitoring, and new capabilities, on your terms.

12
Feedback & Improvement

Guardian Care isn't maintenance — it's momentum. Tell us what's clunky or missing, and feedback becomes features. Your gap becomes everyone's upgrade.

Trustline is small by design — a handful of estates and one architect — so the roadmap is shaped directly by the households using it. Once you're settled in, we check in monthly: a quick "what's working, what isn't." No obligation, no forms. Your friction points are the most valuable data we have, and fixing them is what Guardian Care actually buys.

Why the sequence matters

Order stops mistakes.

We don't wing it. We follow a path. First we plan, then we build, then we move through The Setup Week. Your existing services remain active until the migrated system has been validated and each service is ready to switch over.
