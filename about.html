About Trustline

Own your system. Stop renting your life.

Trustline builds private systems for people who want local control. You get Core Infrastructure, an AI Operator, The Setup Week, and Guardian Care — included through your commissioning period, optional after. No more giving your data to big companies.

Core belief

Sovereignty is not about learning how to manage a server — it's about owning the results. And owning the hardware isn't enough on its own: if you can't control the software, you're still a tenant. A subscription app on your own computer is still someone else's property. Real sovereignty is owning the hardware, controlling the software, and actually being able to use both — which is exactly the combination Trustline builds.

Brand statement

“Big Tech wants you to rent your life. They use corporate kill-switches to lock you out. We build systems you own. No rentals. No switches. Just your data.”

What Trustline is not

More than a tech guy. We are Architects of Sovereignty.

Everyone already knows. The cameras, the tracking, the terms-of-service changes, the subscriptions that quietly become the only option. Everybody sees the direction software ownership is heading — away from owning your apps, your data, even the devices you paid for — and almost nobody likes it. But knowing isn't leverage. The drift from owning to renting won't correct itself, because it's exactly where the incentives point: there is more money in your dependence than in your independence, and integrity doesn't compound. Waiting for it to get better is not a strategy.

The usual escapes don't work either. Self-hosting is genuinely viable — if you're technical. If you're not, the path forks into two dead ends: become an IT person (you won't), or hire one (and you've just built a new chain of dependence with a different landlord at the end of it). That fork is why no real alternative has existed for ordinary households, and why the companies that could build one never will: nobody with scale wants to sell you self-sufficiency, because a customer who doesn't need you again is a terminated revenue stream.

Trustline is built backwards from all of that. The AI Operator is the piece that breaks the fork: a mechanic that lives on the island with you, deeply integrated into your estate, handling the troubleshooting, updates, and management that used to require a technical person — so a non-technical household can genuinely run its own digital life. Our business model is making sure you never need us again. Your estate runs without us, survives without us, and is documented so anyone could take over. Guardian Care exists for households that want our continued work — improvement shipped to your estate, never dependence engineered into it. Cancel any time; your system won't notice.
