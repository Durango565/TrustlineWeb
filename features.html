Technical Blueprint

The Sovereign Stack.

For those who want to see under the hood. While the AI Operator removes the friction, the foundation is built on industry-standard, open-source, and durable infrastructure.

Design Philosophy

We prioritize stability over novelty. Every tool in the stack is chosen for its ability to be self-hosted, audited, and maintained without corporate dependency.

Foundation

Sovereign Core Specs.

The baseline infrastructure that ensures your data is safe, private, and always available.

OS Baseline

Fedora Server: A stable, enterprise-grade Linux foundation with a focus on security and long-term maintainability.

Storage Architecture

RAID 1 / ZFS: Mirrored disks to ensure that a single hardware failure does not result in data loss.

Network Privacy

WireGuard VPN: Secure, high-performance tunnels for remote access. No open ports, no public exposure.

DNS & Filtering

Pi-hole / AdGuard Home: Network-wide ad blocking and tracker prevention at the DNS level.

Data Sync

Nextcloud: A full replacement for Google Drive/Dropbox, handling files, calendars, and contacts privately.

Backup Posture

3-2-1 Strategy: Local snapshots, off-site encrypted copies, and verified restore paths.

Hardware & Intelligence Baselines

Intelligence Scaling.

Local AI capability is primarily driven by Video RAM (VRAM). We scale the hardware to match the reasoning power and context window required for your estate.
