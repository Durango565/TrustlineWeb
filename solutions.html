The Estate

What does "Total Ownership" actually mean?

It means your most important digital assets are no longer held hostage by a corporate subscription.

Your Photos

No more iCloud or Google Photos. Your memories live on your own disks, organized and accessible, but private.

Your Files

Stop paying for Dropbox or Google Drive. Your documents live in your house, not a data center in Virginia.

Your Time

Your calendar and contacts are yours. No tracking. No "smart" suggestions based on spying on your life.

Your Home

Smart devices and cameras run on your estate, not on someone else's server. Home Assistant orchestrates your compatible smart devices today, and camera detection (Frigate) is available on your estate — scoped during your assessment — so there's no monthly camera subscription, no cloud recordings, no accounts, and no company that can hand your footage to anyone on request.

If you own a video doorbell today, the footage of every visitor to your home is stored on a corporate server, subject to disclosure to law enforcement, insurance companies, or anyone else the platform decides to share with. On your Trustline estate, the footage sits on your storage. It stays there. Nobody else can access it — including us.

Living With Your Estate

What this actually looks like day to day.

In your home

A compact, quiet unit that lives wherever your router does — a closet, a shelf, a corner of the office. It hums along at a whisper, sips power, and keeps working through internet outages. This is not a server room; it's an appliance.

On your devices

You keep using the phone, laptop, and apps you already have. Photos back up from your phone automatically. Files sync across your devices. Your calendar and contacts stay in your normal calendar and contacts apps. Away from home, your private VPN connects you securely — everything works from anywhere, exactly as it did before. The difference isn't how it feels. It's who owns it.

When something breaks

You ask your AI Operator first — "is everything okay?" — and it diagnoses, explains, and fixes within its tested skills. For anything deeper, Guardian Care puts a human who knows your exact system one message away. You are never left googling error messages at midnight.

Have a technical friend vetting this? Send them to Under the Hood — the full stack, named, with every boundary explained.

AI Power Tiers

Choose the right private AI capacity.

Every estate starts with Core Infrastructure. The AI Power Tier sizes the private AI hardware to your household. See pricing.

Essential

Supports one AI conversation at a time — the baseline for the AI Server Operator and app functionality to run well. A utility card keeps a lightweight backup model available and handles camera detection when your household adds cameras.

Household

Supports two to three concurrent users. The Operator stays always-on while family members chat, draft, and research simultaneously.

Estate

Supports three or more concurrent users with two tiers of capable AI — a full-size model and a second high-capability model on paired GPUs — plus the utility card. Nothing queues.
