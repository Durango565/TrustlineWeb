What determines the final quote

Scope, migration, and operator depth.

Environment complexity

Network conditions, storage needs, cameras, smart-home scope, phone and endpoint count, and the number of services being consolidated all affect deployment effort.

Migration requirements

The final quote depends heavily on how much data, media, user access, photos, mobile sync behavior, and workflow history must be moved cleanly into the new stack.

Architecture scope

VPN-mediated remote access, DNS and ad-blocking design, backup posture, restore planning, and compatibility constraints materially change the final system design.

Operator capability

Local AI hardware targets, workflow depth, approval boundaries, and premium worker-layer requirements materially change the final system design.
